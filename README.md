# Mint List 🛒[![Download APK](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-APK-success?style=for-the-badge&logo=android)](https://github.com/snhrnck/Mint-List-for-shopping/releases/latest)
[![License: GPL v3](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-GPLv3-blue.svg?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0)

Mint List is a lightweight, minimalist Android application that helps you organize your shopping. Create lists, mark items as bought, and keep a history of your purchases. No internet, no ads, no tracking — just pure focus on your errands.

## ✨ Features

- **📝 Smart Lists** — Add, edit, and check off items with a single tap. Swipe to delete.
- **🗂 History & Cloning** — Close completed lists and clone them for weekly grocery runs.
- **🌙 Dark & Light Themes** — A pleasant pastel design that saves your eyes and battery.
- **🔒 Privacy First** — No internet permission required, no analytics, and no data collection. Everything stays on your device.
- **48 Languages** — Fully localized interface with support for 48 languages, including a detailed user guide.
